Constitution of India — Article 21 (Custodial Liberty & Procedural Due Process)

Jurisdiction: Supreme Court & High Courts • Classification: Inter-Bench Discordance Matrix • Coram Scope: 5-Judge Constitution Bench vs Division Benches

I. Executive Jurisprudential Summary

This monograph synthesizes persistent doctrinal divergences across High Courts concerning custodial detention durations, default bail entitlements, and statutory bail embargoes tested against the inviolable guarantees of Article 21. While the Supreme Court of India maintains strict adherence to substantive due process formulated in the Maneka Gandhi and K.S. Puttaswamy doctrines, state High Courts exhibit irreconcilable divergence when harmonizing procedural penal non-obstante clauses with the indefeasible right to personal freedom under CrPC Section 167(2).
