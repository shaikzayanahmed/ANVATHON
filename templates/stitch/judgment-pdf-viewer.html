32. Mr. Tushar Mehta, learned Additional Solicitor General appearing on behalf of the Union, placed vehement reliance upon the doctrine of harmonious construction. It was urged that procedural limitations embedded in general codes cannot override the express intention of the sovereign Parliament when enacting special statutes aimed at curbing high-order cross-jurisdictional subversions.

1214

33. In counter, the learned Amicus Curiae submitted that personal liberty under Article 21 constitutes the sacrosanct bedrock of our constitutional order. Any detention in police custody extending beyond the normative fortnight recognized by general procedural statutes must undergo strict constitutional scrutiny, failing which the mandate of liberty suffers irreparable derogation.

34. The rigours of constitutional liberty must yield to proportionate custodial scrutiny where statutory enactments provide express exclusion of general procedural defaults. Consequently, the Magistrate possessed full competency to extend remand periods beyond the initial 15-day window without violating the constitutional baseline recognized in general procedural statutes. The petitioner’s reliance on ordinary bail guidelines under Section 167(2) of the Code is thus distinguished and held inapplicable to the case at hand; the special enactment constitutes a standalone procedural code for the duration of custodial interrogation.

35. We must nonetheless reiterate that prolonged detention without commencement of formal charge sheet framing cannot be normalized into a standard inquisitorial instrument. Judicial officers must record explicit objective grounds when granting consecutive extensions under the specialized schedule.

36. In view of the aforesaid findings, the reference is answered in the affirmative. The registry is directed to list the respective individual bail applications before the roster benches for adjudication in light of this settled ratio.
