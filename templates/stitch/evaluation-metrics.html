Formal Conflict Definition

Mathematical & constitutional doctrine for contradiction classification.

“Two judicial propositions PA and PB form a CONFLICT if and only if:

  1. Statutory Alignment: Both cite identical or pari materia constitutional or statutory provisions;
  2. Normative Opposition: Their normative operational holdings dictate diametrically opposing procedural or substantive directives on identical factual predicates; and
  3. Stare Decisis Invariance: Neither judgment has been subsequently overruled or modified by a superior or larger Constitution Bench.
