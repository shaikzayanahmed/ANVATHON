Grounding Target Paragraph 32

[32] To be noted, the expression “inform him of the grounds” in Section 19(1) of the Act of 2002 must be read contextually with Article 22(1) of the Constitution of India. The purpose of informing the arrestee is not merely ceremonial; it is to enable him to seek legal counsel and present a meaningful challenge to his detention. We hold that it is mandatory that a copy of written grounds of arrest be furnished to the arrested person as a matter of course and without exception. Oral recitation does not satisfy the constitutional guarantee enshrined in Article 22(1).
